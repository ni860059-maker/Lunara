05

Marketing Strategy

How the UI supports product visibility

The shawl is always the hero: it is the largest image on the product screen and changes colour instantly. The Raya banner sits at the top of Home. Berry rose is used only for buying actions, so eyes go straight to "Shop Now" and "Buy Now".

Optimised user flow

Every screen has one clear next step. Splash to checkout takes only 4 taps. Sticky buttons, local payment options and a free-shipping progress bar remove friction, and the order-placed screen leads to reviews, turning one purchase into a loop.

Unique features & strategies
  • AR Try-On builds buying confidence
  • Style Finder personalises picks
  • Weather tips make the app feel local
  • Free tutorials show how to wear it
  • Lunara Points reward shared photos
