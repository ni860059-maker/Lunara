## 1. Product concept and branding

**Product:** Lunara makes breathable, no-iron chiffon and cotton shawls for all-day wear in Malaysia's heat. A non-slip weave means fewer pins and a neater look.

- Breathable chiffon and cotton voile
- No-iron, wrinkle-free finish
- Non-slip weave, so fewer pins
- 5+ colours per design, priced RM39–RM59

**Tagline:** *"Light drape, all-day grace."*

**Logo:** a crescent moon (calm, modest elegance) with a small star (the shine of a confident look), next to the lowercase wordmark "lunara".

| Colour | Hex | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Deep Plum | `#4A2C40` | Headers, logo, navigation |
| Dusty Rose | `#E8C4C4` | Cards, highlights |
| Berry Rose | `#B03A5B` | Buy buttons only |
| Ivory | `#FBF7F2` | Backgrounds |

**Typeface:** Poppins. Bold for headings, Regular for body text.

## 2. Target audience and persona

**Target audience:** Muslim women aged 18–35, mainly students and working women, who find hijab styling ideas on TikTok and Instagram.

**Persona: Farah Nadhirah, 24.** Junior executive in Kuala Lumpur who commutes by LRT and spends about RM40–60 per shawl.

> "I don't have time to re-pin my shawl at work. It has to stay neat and not feel hot."

| Goals | Frustrations | Habits |
|---|---|---|
| Look neat from 8am to 6pm | Shawls that slip all day | Watches hijab tutorials on TikTok |
| Stay cool in humid weather | Thick fabric that feels hot | Shops on her phone at night |
| Try new styles quickly | Colours that look different online | Trusts real customer photos |

**Planned audience split (concept):** working women 23–30 (45%), students 18–22 (35%), young mothers 31–35 (20%).

**Where we reach her:** TikTok styling tutorials, Instagram Reels and hijab influencers, Raya campaigns that drive app downloads, campus and office pop-up booths.

## 3. UI mock-up (5 screens)

| # | Screen | Role | Key design choices |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Splash | Sets the mood | Plum background and crescent logo build brand recall; three benefit tags explain "why Lunara"; one clear **Get Started** button |
| 2 | Home | Discover | Personal greeting and weather tip suggest the right fabric; Raya sale banner sits where eyes land first; Style Finder quiz guides unsure shoppers |
| 3 | Product Details | Decide | Large shawl image changes with every colour tap; **Try it on (AR)** removes the "will it suit me?" doubt; sticky Add to Cart stays within thumb reach |
| 4 | Checkout (CTA) | Act | Berry-rose **Buy Now** is the only bright button; brooch add-on and free-shipping bar raise order value; FPX, Touch 'n Go, DuitNow and card feel familiar |
| 5 | Reviews | Trust | 4.9-star summary and Verified Buyer tags build trust; filters help users find people like them; points reward customers for sharing their look |

**UI/UX principles applied:** buttons at least 44px tall plus bottom navigation for one-handed use; one CTA colour (berry rose) used only for buying actions; a single typeface with a clear heading-to-body hierarchy; high-contrast, accessible text; the same palette, icons and logo throughout.

## 4. User flow

**Discover** (Home banner, weather tip) → **Find** (60-sec Style Finder) → **Try** (pick a colour, AR try-on) → **Buy** (checkout in a few taps) → **Share** (post a review, earn points)

**The loop:** Lunara Points and new collections bring her back to Discover.

## 5. Marketing strategy

**How the UI supports product visibility.** The shawl is always the hero: it is the largest image on the product screen and changes colour instantly. The Raya banner sits at the top of Home. Berry rose is used only for buying actions, so eyes go straight to "Shop Now" and "Buy Now".

**Optimised user flow.** Every screen has one clear next step. Splash to checkout takes only 4 taps. Sticky buttons, local payment options and a free-shipping progress bar remove friction, and the order-placed screen leads to reviews, turning one purchase into a loop.

**Unique features and strategies.**
- **AR Try-On** builds buying confidence
- **Style Finder** personalises picks
- **Weather tips** make the app feel local
- **Free tutorials** show how to wear what she bought
- **Lunara Points** reward shared customer photos

Tactics used: personalisation, social proof, urgency (Raya sale), upselling (magnetic brooch) and gamification (points).
